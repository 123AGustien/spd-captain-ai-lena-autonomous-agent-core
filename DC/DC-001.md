# DC-001 — Cooling Failure

## Rule Information

Rule ID: DC-001  
Domain: Data Centre Resilience  
Category: Cooling Infrastructure  
Status: Active  
Version: 1.0  
Repository: Sextant Rule Library

## Purpose

Evaluate data centre resilience during cooling system failure and assess potential thermal escalation, service degradation, and cascading infrastructure impacts.

## Scenario

A data centre cooling system experiences a failure resulting in rising thermal conditions and increased risk to computing infrastructure.

The scenario evaluates:

- Cooling system availability
- Rack temperature stability
- Thermal threshold escalation
- Backup cooling availability
- Compute workload response
- Hardware thermal protection

## Trigger Conditions

- Cooling unit failure detected
- Temperature rising beyond safe threshold
- CRAC or chiller alarm activated

## Primary Indicators

- Rack temperature increase
- Cooling system telemetry alerts
- Power usage spike from compensatory cooling or load
- Hardware thermal warnings
- Cooling capacity availability
- Thermal threshold status

## Risk Levels

### GREEN

Normal temperature and cooling stability.

Cooling systems are operating normally and adequate thermal headroom is available.

No contingency actions required.

### YELLOW

Minor cooling inefficiency detected.

Monitor:

- Temperature trends
- Cooling system performance
- Thermal headroom
- Cooling alarms

Action:

Increase monitoring and verify backup cooling readiness.

### ORANGE

Sustained temperature rise or partial cooling failure.

Indicators:

- Increasing rack temperatures
- Reduced cooling capacity
- Localised thermal hotspots
- Cooling system instability

Potential Cascade:

Cooling Failure  
↓  
Temperature Rise  
↓  
Server Throttling  
↓  
System Performance Degradation

Action:

Activate available backup cooling, reduce non-critical compute workload, shift critical services where appropriate, and continuously monitor thermal thresholds.

### RED

Critical cooling failure with immediate thermal risk to hardware.

Indicators:

- Rapid temperature escalation
- Major cooling capacity loss
- Persistent thermal hotspots
- Hardware thermal protection activation
- Risk of hardware shutdown

Potential Cascade:

Cooling Failure  
↓  
Temperature Rise  
↓  
Server Throttling  
↓  
System Performance Degradation  
↓  
Potential Hardware Shutdown  
↓  
Cross-Domain Systemic Risk

Action:

Initiate emergency thermal protection, activate backup cooling systems, reduce non-critical workloads, protect critical services, and escalate to incident management.

## Cross-Domain Dependencies

↓

↓

Cooling failure may affect infrastructure availability, computing capacity, cybersecurity monitoring, digital services, and financial systems dependent on the data centre.

## Contingency Actions

- Activate backup cooling systems
- Reduce non-critical compute workload
- Shift critical services where available
- Notify operations team
- Monitor thermal thresholds continuously
- Protect critical infrastructure
- Escalate when thermal limits are exceeded

## Recovery Actions

- Restore primary cooling systems
- Verify cooling stability
- Rebalance workloads
- Verify hardware integrity
- Confirm thermal conditions are within safe limits
- Resume normal operations progressively
