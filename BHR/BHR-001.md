# SPD v13.1 — BHR-001
## Labour Rights Resilience Rule

### Rule ID
BHR-001

### Domain
BHR — Business & Human Rights Resilience

### Scenario
LABOUR_RIGHTS

### Rule Name
Labour Rights Risk

---

## Purpose

Detect and assess labour-rights-related resilience stress within an
organisation, operation, project, supplier, or affected workforce.

The rule provides deterministic decision support for:

- labour-rights concerns
- worker welfare risks
- unsafe working conditions
- labour governance deficiencies
- workforce-related escalation
- potential operational and human-rights impacts

---

## Input Indicators

The rule may evaluate:

- `labour`
- `humanRights`
- `governance`
- `community`
- `supplyChain`
- `environment`
- `intensity`

All values are normalised to the range:

`0–100`

---

## Evaluation Principle

Higher indicator values represent greater identified risk or stress.

The BHR Rule Engine calculates a deterministic BHR stress score and
resilience score.

Human-rights and labour indicators receive significant weighting within
the BHR assessment model.

Scenario intensity acts as an additional stress multiplier.

---

## Risk Classification

| Stress | Risk |
|---:|---|
| `< 40` | LOW |
| `40–69.999` | MEDIUM |
| `≥ 70` | HIGH |

---

## Expected Decision Support

### LOW

Recommended posture:

`MAINTAIN_MONITORING`

Human authority:

Monitoring only.

### MEDIUM

Recommended posture:

`INITIATE_BHR_MITIGATION_REVIEW`

Human authority:

Human authorization required before execution.

### HIGH

Recommended posture:

`ESCALATE_BHR_RISK_AND_MAINTAIN_SAFE_STATE`

Human authority:

Human authorization required before execution.

---

## Potential Cascade

A significant labour-rights event may develop through:

1. Labour Rights Concern
2. Workforce Impact
3. Operational Disruption
4. Governance / Compliance Exposure
5. Cross-Domain Resilience Risk

---

## Decision-Support Actions

Potential actions include:

- review labour conditions
- verify worker-safety controls
- assess workforce impact
- review labour governance
- assess affected suppliers
- increase monitoring
- escalate according to governance procedures

Actions are recommendations only.

---

## Governance

AI provides decision support.

`HUMAN_OPERATOR` retains execution authority.

No autonomous recovery or enforcement action is permitted.

Execution status must remain:

`HUMAN_AUTHORIZATION_REQUIRED`

where an operational action is recommended.

---

## Audit Requirements

The evaluation should record:

- domain
- rule ID
- scenario
- calculated stress
- resilience score
- risk classification
- recommended decision
- execution authority
- execution status
- timestamp

---

## Deterministic Requirement

For identical validated inputs, the rule must produce identical:

- stress
- resilience score
- risk classification
- decision classification

Timestamp generation may vary between executions and is audit metadata.
