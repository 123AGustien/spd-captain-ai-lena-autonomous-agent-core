# SPD v13.1 — BHR-010
## Retaliation & Grievance Mechanism Risk Resilience Rule

### Rule ID
BHR-010

### Domain
BHR — Business & Human Rights Resilience

### Scenario
RETALIATION_GRIEVANCE_RISK

### Rule Name
Retaliation & Grievance Mechanism Risk

---

## Purpose

Detect and assess potential risks associated with retaliation, ineffective
grievance mechanisms, inadequate reporting channels, or barriers to raising
business and human-rights concerns.

The rule provides deterministic decision support for:

- retaliation indicators
- grievance mechanism weakness
- worker vulnerability
- human-rights exposure
- community concerns
- governance weaknesses
- supply-chain exposure
- escalation requirements

The assessment is a risk indicator only and does not constitute a legal
finding.

---

## Input Indicators

The rule may evaluate:

- `labour`
- `humanRights`
- `community`
- `governance`
- `supplyChain`
- `environment`
- `intensity`

All values are normalised to:

`0–100`

Higher values represent greater assessed risk or stress.

---

## Evaluation Principle

Retaliation and grievance-mechanism risk is evaluated together with
labour, human-rights, community, governance, supply-chain, and
environmental indicators.

Scenario intensity acts as an additional deterministic stress modifier.

Particular attention is given to the protection of individuals and
communities raising concerns.

### HIGH

---

## Potential Cascade

A significant retaliation or grievance risk may develop through:

1. Concern or Grievance Raised
2. Reporting / Grievance Mechanism Weakness
3. Retaliation or Worker / Community Vulnerability
4. Human-Rights and Governance Exposure
5. Cross-Domain Systemic Risk

---

## Decision-Support Actions

Potential actions include:

- review grievance mechanisms
- assess reporting-channel accessibility
- verify confidentiality and protection safeguards
- assess retaliation indicators
- preserve relevant records and evidence
- increase independent monitoring
- review governance controls
- escalate material concerns through appropriate authority

Actions are recommendations only.

---

## Governance

`HUMAN_OPERATOR` retains execution authority.

determination, employment action, contract termination, remediation, or
operational recovery action is permitted.

`HUMAN_AUTHORIZATION_REQUIRED`
