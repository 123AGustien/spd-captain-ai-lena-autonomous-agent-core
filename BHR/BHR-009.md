# SPD v13.1 — BHR-009
## Discrimination & Equal Treatment Risk Resilience Rule

### Rule ID
BHR-009

### Domain
BHR — Business & Human Rights Resilience

### Scenario
DISCRIMINATION_RISK

### Rule Name
Discrimination & Equal Treatment Risk

---

## Purpose

Detect and assess potential discrimination and unequal-treatment risks
within organisational operations, employment practices, supply chains,
contractor relationships, or affected communities.

The rule provides deterministic decision support for:

- discrimination indicators
- equal-treatment concerns
- labour-rights exposure
- human-rights impact
- community impact
- governance weaknesses
- supply-chain exposure
- escalation requirements

The assessment is a risk indicator only and does not constitute a legal
finding.

---

## Input Indicators

The rule may evaluate:

- `labour`
- `humanRights`
- `community`
- `governance`
- `supplyChain`
- `environment`
- `intensity`

All values are normalised to:

`0–100`

Higher values represent greater assessed risk or stress.

---

## Evaluation Principle

Discrimination and equal-treatment risk is evaluated together with
labour, human-rights, community, governance, supply-chain, and
environmental indicators.

Scenario intensity acts as an additional deterministic stress modifier.

---

## Risk Classification

| Stress | Risk |
|---:|---|
| `< 40` | LOW |
| `40–69.999` | MEDIUM |
| `≥ 70` | HIGH |

---

## Expected Decision Support

### LOW

Recommended posture:

`MAINTAIN_MONITORING`

### MEDIUM

Recommended posture:

`INITIATE_BHR_MITIGATION_REVIEW`

Human authorization is required before operational execution.

### HIGH

Recommended posture:

`ESCALATE_BHR_RISK_AND_MAINTAIN_SAFE_STATE`

Human authorization is required before operational execution.

---

## Potential Cascade

A significant discrimination risk may develop through:

1. Discrimination Indicator
2. Unequal Treatment / Worker or Community Impact
3. Human-Rights and Labour Exposure
4. Governance and Operational Impact
5. Cross-Domain Systemic Risk

---

## Decision-Support Actions

Potential actions include:

- review equal-treatment controls
- assess affected worker or stakeholder groups
- review relevant policies and procedures
- verify grievance and reporting mechanisms
- assess supplier and contractor practices
- increase monitoring
- escalate material concerns through governance procedures

Actions are recommendations only.

---

## Governance

AI provides decision support.

`HUMAN_OPERATOR` retains execution authority.

No autonomous disciplinary action, employment decision, enforcement,
contract termination, remediation, or operational recovery action is
permitted.

Operational execution must remain subject to:

`HUMAN_AUTHORIZATION_REQUIRED`

- stress
