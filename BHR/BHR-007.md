# SPD v13.1 — BHR-007
## Forced Labour Risk Resilience Rule

### Rule ID
BHR-007

### Domain
BHR — Business & Human Rights Resilience

### Scenario
FORCED_LABOUR_RISK

### Rule Name
Forced Labour Risk

---

## Purpose

Detect and assess resilience risks associated with potential forced-labour
conditions within organisational operations, suppliers, contractors, or
other business relationships.

The rule provides deterministic decision support for:

- forced-labour indicators
- worker vulnerability
- labour-rights exposure
- supply-chain exposure
- human-rights impact
- governance weaknesses
- escalation requirements

---

## Input Indicators

The rule may evaluate:

- `labour`
- `humanRights`
- `supplyChain`
- `governance`
- `community`
- `environment`
- `intensity`

All values are normalised to:

`0–100`

Higher values represent greater assessed risk or stress.

---

## Evaluation Principle

Forced-labour risk is evaluated as a serious business and human-rights
resilience indicator.

The assessment considers labour, human-rights, supply-chain, governance,
community, and environmental factors.

Scenario intensity acts as an additional deterministic stress modifier.

The result is decision support and does not constitute a legal finding
that forced labour has occurred.

---

## Risk Classification

| Stress | Risk |
|---:|---|
| `< 40` | LOW |
| `40–69.999` | MEDIUM |
| `≥ 70` | HIGH |

---

## Expected Decision Support

### LOW

Recommended posture:

`MAINTAIN_MONITORING`

### MEDIUM

Recommended posture:

`INITIATE_BHR_MITIGATION_REVIEW`

Human authorization is required before operational execution.

### HIGH

Recommended posture:

`ESCALATE_BHR_RISK_AND_MAINTAIN_SAFE_STATE`

Human authorization is required before operational execution.

---

## Potential Cascade

A significant forced-labour risk may develop through:

1. Forced Labour Indicator
2. Worker Vulnerability
3. Labour / Human Rights Impact
4. Supply-Chain and Governance Exposure
5. Cross-Domain Systemic Risk

---

## Decision-Support Actions

Potential actions include:

- assess worker conditions
- review labour practices and safeguards
- verify supplier and contractor controls
- conduct appropriate human-rights due diligence
- increase monitoring
- preserve relevant evidence and records
- escalate material concerns through governance procedures

Actions are recommendations only.

---

## Governance

AI provides decision support.

`HUMAN_OPERATOR` retains execution authority.

No autonomous enforcement, worker-management action, contract
termination, remediation, or operational recovery action is permitted.

Operational execution must remain subject to:

`HUMAN_AUTHORIZATION_REQUIRED`
