# SPD v13.1 — BHR-004
## Community Impact Resilience Rule

### Rule ID
BHR-004

### Domain
BHR — Business & Human Rights Resilience

### Scenario
COMMUNITY_IMPACT

### Rule Name
Community Impact Risk

---

## Purpose

Detect and assess resilience risks arising from actual or simulated
impacts on communities affected by organisational activities,
operations, projects, infrastructure, or supply-chain activities.

The rule provides deterministic decision support for:

- community impact
- affected-person risk
- human-rights concerns
- labour-related effects
- governance exposure
- environmental effects
- operational escalation

---

## Input Indicators

The rule may evaluate:

- `community`
- `humanRights`
- `labour`
- `environment`
- `governance`
- `supplyChain`
- `intensity`

All values are normalised to:

`0–100`

Higher values represent greater assessed risk or stress.

---

## Evaluation Principle

The rule evaluates community impact together with related human-rights,
labour, environmental, governance, and supply-chain indicators.

Scenario intensity increases the calculated stress through the
deterministic BHR assessment model.

---

## Risk Classification

| Stress | Risk |
|---:|---|
| `< 40` | LOW |
| `40–69.999` | MEDIUM |
| `≥ 70` | HIGH |

---

## Expected Decision Support

### LOW

Recommended posture:

`MAINTAIN_MONITORING`

### MEDIUM

Recommended posture:

`INITIATE_BHR_MITIGATION_REVIEW`

Human authorization is required before operational execution.

### HIGH

Recommended posture:

`ESCALATE_BHR_RISK_AND_MAINTAIN_SAFE_STATE`

Human authorization is required before operational execution.

---

## Potential Cascade

A significant community-impact event may develop through:

1. Community Impact
2. Affected-Person / Stakeholder Impact
3. Human-Rights or Environmental Concern
4. Operational and Governance Disruption
5. Cross-Domain Resilience Risk

---

## Decision-Support Actions

Potential actions include:

- assess affected communities
- verify available safeguards
- review human-rights impacts
- assess environmental implications
- review stakeholder and governance processes
- increase monitoring
- escalate material concerns through appropriate authority

Actions are recommendations only.

---

## Governance

AI provides decision support.

`HUMAN_OPERATOR` retains execution authority.

No autonomous enforcement, remediation, compensation, operational
shutdown, or recovery action is permitted.

Operational execution must remain subject to:

`HUMAN_AUTHORIZATION_REQUIRED`

---

## Audit Requirements

The evaluation should record:

- domain
- rule ID
- scenario
- input state
- stress
- resilience score
- risk classification
- identified BHR concerns
- recommended decision
- execution authority
- execution status
- timestamp

---

## Deterministic Requirement

Identical validated inputs must produce identical:

- stress
- resilience score
- risk classification
- decision classification

Timestamp values are audit metadata and may differ between executions.
