# SPD v13.1 — BHR-005
## Governance Risk Resilience Rule

### Rule ID
BHR-005

### Domain
BHR — Business & Human Rights Resilience

### Scenario
GOVERNANCE_RISK

### Rule Name
Business & Human Rights Governance Risk

---

## Purpose

Detect and assess governance weaknesses that may increase business and
human-rights exposure.

The rule provides deterministic decision support for:

- governance-control weakness
- human-rights oversight gaps
- labour governance exposure
- supply-chain governance risk
- community-impact governance
- environmental governance
- escalation requirements

---

## Input Indicators

The rule may evaluate:

- `governance`
- `humanRights`
- `labour`
- `supplyChain`
- `community`
- `environment`
- `intensity`

All values are normalised to:

`0–100`

Higher values represent greater assessed risk or stress.

---

## Evaluation Principle

Governance is treated as a central BHR resilience indicator.

The rule evaluates governance exposure together with human-rights,
labour, supply-chain, community, and environmental indicators.

Scenario intensity is applied as an additional deterministic stress
modifier.

---

## Risk Classification

| Stress | Risk |
|---:|---|
| `< 40` | LOW |
| `40–69.999` | MEDIUM |
| `≥ 70` | HIGH |

---

## Expected Decision Support

### LOW

Recommended posture:

`MAINTAIN_MONITORING`

### MEDIUM

Recommended posture:

`INITIATE_BHR_MITIGATION_REVIEW`

Human authorization is required before operational execution.

### HIGH

Recommended posture:

`ESCALATE_BHR_RISK_AND_MAINTAIN_SAFE_STATE`

Human authorization is required before operational execution.

---

## Potential Cascade

A significant governance event may develop through:

1. Governance Control Weakness
2. BHR Oversight Gap
3. Increased Human-Rights Exposure
4. Operational / Compliance Risk
5. Cross-Domain Systemic Risk

---

## Decision-Support Actions

Potential actions include:

- review BHR governance controls
- verify management oversight
- assess policy and control effectiveness
- review supplier and contractor governance
- strengthen monitoring and reporting
- identify unresolved BHR risks
- escalate material governance concerns

Actions are recommendations only.

---

## Governance

AI provides decision support.

`HUMAN_OPERATOR` retains execution authority.

No autonomous governance decision, enforcement action, disciplinary
action, contract termination, or remediation action is permitted.

Operational execution must remain subject to:

`HUMAN_AUTHORIZATION_REQUIRED`
