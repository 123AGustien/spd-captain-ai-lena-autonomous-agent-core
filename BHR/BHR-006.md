# SPD v13.1 — BHR-006
## Environmental Human Rights Impact Resilience Rule

### Rule ID
BHR-006

### Domain
BHR — Business & Human Rights Resilience

### Scenario
ENVIRONMENTAL_HUMAN_RIGHTS_IMPACT

### Rule Name
Environmental Human Rights Impact

---

## Purpose

Detect and assess resilience risks where environmental conditions may
create or contribute to adverse business and human-rights impacts.

The rule provides deterministic decision support for:

- environmental impact
- community exposure
- human-rights impact
- labour and workforce exposure
- supply-chain consequences
- governance weaknesses
- escalation requirements

---

## Input Indicators

The rule may evaluate:

- `environment`
- `humanRights`
- `community`
- `labour`
- `supplyChain`
- `governance`
- `intensity`

All values are normalised to:

`0–100`

Higher values represent greater assessed risk or stress.

---

## Evaluation Principle

Environmental impact is evaluated together with associated human-rights,
community, labour, supply-chain, and governance indicators.

The scenario intensity value acts as an additional deterministic stress
modifier.

The assessment is decision support only and does not constitute a
legal or regulatory determination.

---

## Risk Classification

| Stress | Risk |
|---:|---|
| `< 40` | LOW |
| `40–69.999` | MEDIUM |
| `≥ 70` | HIGH |

---

## Expected Decision Support

### LOW

Recommended posture:

`MAINTAIN_MONITORING`

### MEDIUM

Recommended posture:

`INITIATE_BHR_MITIGATION_REVIEW`

Human authorization is required before operational execution.

### HIGH

Recommended posture:

`ESCALATE_BHR_RISK_AND_MAINTAIN_SAFE_STATE`

Human authorization is required before operational execution.

---

## Potential Cascade

A significant environmental human-rights event may develop through:

1. Environmental Impact
2. Community Exposure
3. Human-Rights Concern
4. Operational / Supply-Chain Impact
5. Cross-Domain Systemic Risk

---

## Decision-Support Actions

Potential actions include:

- assess environmental exposure
- identify affected communities
- review human-rights safeguards
- assess workforce implications
- review affected suppliers or contractors
- strengthen environmental and BHR monitoring
- escalate material concerns through governance procedures

Actions are recommendations only.

---

## Governance

AI provides decision support.

`HUMAN_OPERATOR` retains execution authority.

No autonomous enforcement, remediation, shutdown, compensation,
contract termination, or recovery action is permitted.

Operational execution must remain subject to:

`HUMAN_AUTHORIZATION_REQUIRED`
