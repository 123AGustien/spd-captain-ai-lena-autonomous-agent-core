# SPD v13.1 — BHR-003
## Supply Chain Human Rights Resilience Rule

### Rule ID
BHR-003

### Domain
BHR — Business & Human Rights Resilience

### Scenario
SUPPLY_CHAIN_HUMAN_RIGHTS

### Rule Name
Supply Chain Human Rights Risk

---

## Purpose

Detect and assess human-rights resilience risks arising within
suppliers, contractors, subcontractors, logistics providers, or other
external business relationships.

The rule provides deterministic decision support for:

- supplier labour conditions
- human-rights exposure
- supply-chain governance
- community impacts
- contractor risk
- escalation of material supply-chain concerns

---

## Input Indicators

The rule may evaluate:

- `supplyChain`
- `labour`
- `humanRights`
- `governance`
- `community`
- `environment`
- `intensity`

All values are normalised to:

`0–100`

Higher values represent greater assessed risk or stress.

---

## Evaluation Principle

The rule evaluates supply-chain human-rights exposure together with
related labour, human-rights, governance, community, and environmental
indicators.

Scenario intensity acts as an additional stress modifier within the
deterministic BHR assessment model.

---

## Risk Classification

| Stress | Risk |
|---:|---|
| `< 40` | LOW |
| `40–69.999` | MEDIUM |
| `≥ 70` | HIGH |

---

## Expected Decision Support

### LOW

Recommended posture:

`MAINTAIN_MONITORING`

### MEDIUM

Recommended posture:

`INITIATE_BHR_MITIGATION_REVIEW`

Human authorization is required before operational execution.

### HIGH

Recommended posture:

`ESCALATE_BHR_RISK_AND_MAINTAIN_SAFE_STATE`

Human authorization is required before operational execution.

---

## Potential Cascade

A significant supply-chain human-rights event may develop through:

1. Supplier Risk Identification
2. Labour / Human Rights Impact
3. Supply Disruption
4. Governance and Compliance Exposure
5. Cross-Domain Systemic Risk

---

## Decision-Support Actions

Potential actions include:

- review supplier human-rights controls
- assess affected workers
- verify supplier governance
- review contractor dependencies
- increase supply-chain monitoring
- assess alternative supply arrangements
- escalate material findings through governance channels

Actions are recommendations only.

---

## Governance

AI provides decision support.

`HUMAN_OPERATOR` retains execution authority.

No autonomous supplier enforcement, contract termination,
operational recovery, or remediation action is permitted.

Operational execution must remain subject to:

`HUMAN_AUTHORIZATION_REQUIRED`
