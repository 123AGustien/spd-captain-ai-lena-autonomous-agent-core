# SPD v13.1 — BHR-002
## Human Rights Event Resilience Rule

### Rule ID
BHR-002

### Domain
BHR — Business & Human Rights Resilience

### Scenario
HUMAN_RIGHTS_EVENT

### Rule Name
Human Rights Event

---

## Purpose

Detect and assess resilience stress arising from an identified or
simulated human-rights event affecting people, operations, communities,
suppliers, or organisational activities.

The rule provides deterministic decision support for:

- human-rights impacts
- affected-person risk
- operational consequences
- governance exposure
- supply-chain implications
- community impacts
- escalation requirements

---

## Input Indicators

The rule may evaluate:

- `humanRights`
- `labour`
- `supplyChain`
- `community`
- `governance`
- `environment`
- `intensity`

All values are normalised to:

`0–100`

Higher values represent greater assessed stress.

---

## Evaluation Principle

The rule evaluates the severity of the human-rights event together with
related labour, supply-chain, community, governance, and environmental
indicators.

Scenario intensity increases the resulting stress according to the
deterministic BHR assessment model.

---

## Risk Classification

| Stress | Risk |
|---:|---|
| `< 40` | LOW |
| `40–69.999` | MEDIUM |
| `≥ 70` | HIGH |

---

## Expected Decision Support

### LOW

Recommended posture:

`MAINTAIN_MONITORING`

### MEDIUM

Recommended posture:

`INITIATE_BHR_MITIGATION_REVIEW`

### HIGH

Recommended posture:

`ESCALATE_BHR_RISK_AND_MAINTAIN_SAFE_STATE`

Operational actions require human authorization.

---

## Potential Cascade

A significant human-rights event may develop through:

1. Human Rights Event
2. Affected-Person Impact
3. Operational / Supply-Chain Disruption
4. Governance and Compliance Exposure
5. Cross-Domain Resilience Risk

---

## Decision-Support Actions

Potential actions include:

- assess affected persons and communities
- verify available safeguards
- review operational impacts
- review supplier or contractor exposure
- strengthen monitoring
- escalate governance review
- maintain a safe operational state where required

These are recommendations only.

---

## Governance

AI provides decision support.

`HUMAN_OPERATOR` retains execution authority.

No autonomous enforcement, remediation, or recovery action is permitted.

Execution status for operational actions:

`HUMAN_AUTHORIZATION_REQUIRED`

## Deterministic Requirement

Identical validated inputs must produce identical:

- stress
- resilience score
- risk
- decision classification

The timestamp is audit metadata and may differ between executions.
