# CYB-005 — Ransomware / System Encryption Event

## Rule Information

Rule ID: CYB-005
Domain: Cyber Resilience
Category: Malware / System Availability
Status: Active
Version: 1.0
Repository: Sextant Rule Library

## Purpose

Evaluate system resilience under ransomware or system-encryption conditions and assess cascading impacts across data availability, infrastructure, services, and dependent domains.

## Scenario

Critical systems or data become unavailable due to malicious or simulated encryption activity. The scenario evaluates the ability of the environment to detect the event, isolate affected systems, preserve system integrity, and restore critical services through validated recovery mechanisms.

The scenario evaluates:

- System availability
- Backup integrity
- Recovery capability
- Endpoint isolation effectiveness
- Data integrity
- Recovery coordination
- Business continuity readiness

## Primary Indicators

- Rapid file or storage availability changes
- Abnormal encryption activity
- Endpoint isolation alerts
- Backup integrity status
- Recovery point availability
- Service availability degradation
- Unusual privileged activity

## Risk Levels

### GREEN

No ransomware or abnormal encryption indicators detected.

Systems and backups operate normally.

No contingency actions required.

### YELLOW

Early indicators of suspicious encryption or malware activity.

Monitor:

- Abnormal file activity
- Endpoint alerts
- Backup health
- Unusual authentication activity

Action:

Increase monitoring and verify backup and recovery readiness.

### ORANGE

Confirmed ransomware or system-encryption event affecting a limited portion of the environment.

Indicators:

- Multiple affected systems
- Service degradation
- Suspicious encryption activity
- Increased recovery dependency

Potential Cascade:

Ransomware Event
↓
System Encryption
↓
Service Degradation
↓
Operational Disruption

Action:

Isolate affected systems, protect validated backups, restrict compromised access, and initiate incident response procedures.

### RED

Critical ransomware event affecting multiple critical systems.

Indicators:

- Widespread system unavailability
- Critical service disruption
- Backup or recovery integrity concerns
- Significant infrastructure impact

Potential Cascade:

Ransomware Event
↓
System-Wide Encryption
↓
Service Outage
↓
Infrastructure Disruption
↓
Data Recovery Risk
↓
Cross-Domain Systemic Risk

Action:

Activate emergency containment, isolate affected infrastructure, protect recovery systems, validate clean backups, and initiate disaster recovery procedures.

## Cross-Domain Dependencies

Cyber (CYB)
↓
Data Centre (DC)
↓
Infrastructure (INF)
↓
Financial (FIN)

Ransomware may disrupt data-centre services, infrastructure operations, financial systems, and other dependent services.

## Contingency Actions

- Isolate affected systems
- Restrict compromised accounts and access paths
- Protect backup and recovery infrastructure
- Validate backup integrity
- Preserve forensic and audit evidence
- Restore critical services from verified clean recovery points
- Monitor for continued system instability
- Escalate to incident response and security governance teams
